Connect to your computer

Install Secpar on each computer and on your phone, and sign in with the same account on all of them. Each computer you sign in on appears under Computers on your other devices, with a still of its screen. Select a computer to connect. Under each computer you see who is using it now. The list updates by itself as computers come online and people connect.

Windows and Linux use one app to host and to connect. The Android app connects to computers; it does not host. Up to three devices can stream from one computer at the same time.

Secpar connects directly when it can, on your home network or across the internet, and goes through Secpar's relay when a direct route is blocked.

Below your computers, Connected to this computer shows who is using it now, with Disconnect to end a session. Paired devices lists the devices that may use this computer; click its title to fold it away. Remove stops a device connecting at all.

Friends

Your username is on the Friends tab. To add someone, type their username and select Add. They accept the request on their side. Friends are kept on your account, so they show on every device you sign in on.

For each friend you choose:

  • Ask first or Any time: whether you approve each connection.
  • Keyboard, mouse and files: the three buttons beside the friend. A highlighted button is allowed. Files lets the friend browse your files, not only send to them. Keyboard and mouse changes reach a friend who is already connected within a few seconds.

Your friends' computers appear under Computers. Select one to connect: Secpar asks for whatever its owner allows. With Ask first, the owner approves once, from the notification or from the Secpar window. When a friend starts using your computer, your desktop tells you. Both computers need Secpar 4.0.12 or later and must be signed in to their owners' accounts. If a friend turns off previews, you see their computer without a still of its screen.

During a stream

On a computer, the stream opens in a window shaped like the remote screen at half its size, and the window goes back to its own size when you disconnect. Shortcuts use the release keys set in Settings › Streaming (Ctrl + Alt unless you change them):

  • Release keys + W: full screen or window.
  • Release keys + S: stream menu. You can also select the Secpar triangle at the top left of the stream.
  • Release keys + Q: disconnect.

In full screen the mouse and keyboard go to the remote computer. Send system shortcuts decides whether keys such as Alt + Tab go there too.

When the computer has more than one screen, Switch display in the stream menu picks the one you see. On a computer, Open in a new window under it shows another screen in a second window, so you can see two at once.

On Android, the floating button opens the stream menu: keyboard, trackpad mode, controllers and disconnect. A docked keyboard and mouse work directly.

Stream stats turns the stats bar on or off and shows the sound details. Closing the window on a computer ends the stream.

Files and clipboard

Select the folder button on a computer's tile to browse its files and copy files either way. You can also drop files onto that window to send them.

Text copied on one device can be pasted on the other while you are connected. Turn this off with Share clipboard in Settings › Streaming on the connecting device, or in Settings › This computer on the host.

Android TV

Secpar runs on Android TV and Chromecast with Google TV. It is the same app as on phones, from the same download. Open the .apk on the TV with an app that can install files, such as a file manager, and allow installing from that app when the TV asks. Secpar then appears on the TV home screen. Older Chromecasts without a remote cannot run apps.

Move with the arrows on the remote. The selected item has a green outline, and a selected computer card is outlined as a whole. In a stream, open the stream menu with the Secpar button at the top left. While it is open, the arrows move through it and Back closes it.

The remote moves through Secpar but does not control the computer well. Pair a Bluetooth keyboard and mouse or a controller with the TV for that.

Power

From Android, open the menu on a computer's tile to lock it, put it to sleep, sign out, restart or shut it down. This works on Windows and Linux computers you own. Friends cannot use it.

This computer

Settings › This computer controls hosting on the computer you are using:

  • Picture: a quality limit that caps the resolution and frame rate a device may ask for, the encoder and its preset. NVIDIA graphics on Windows offer presets P1 (fastest) to P7 (best quality). On Windows you can also pick the display. Linux has Show host cursor: turn it off and the connecting device draws the pointer, which follows the mouse without delay but is always an arrow. Windows always works that way.
  • Virtual display (Windows): adds a screen that exists only for streaming, for a computer with no monitor or to stream a second screen. Virtual display size sets its resolution. Turn it off to remove the screen.
  • Who can use it: receiving files, sharing the clipboard and Show desktop preview thumbnail. With it off, no device or friend gets a still of your screen.
  • Start in tray: open Secpar in the tray when you sign in, so this computer can be reached.

Hosting runs while Secpar is open or in the tray. Quit Secpar in the tray menu ends hosting; Restart Secpar starts it again.

Linux hosting

Keep the Linux computer signed in to its desktop. The first time a device connects, your desktop asks whether to allow screen sharing and remote control. On KDE Plasma this is Remote Control Requested. Select Allow, with Allow the application to do this without asking next time ticked, so later connections do not wait for it.

When the Linux computer is locked, the lock screen still shows and you can type your password from your own devices. Friends cannot use the lock screen. This needs write access to /dev/uinput, the same access controllers use.

The AppImage runs on most x86-64 distributions. Run it once with --install to add Secpar to your app menu. Router ports (UPnP) are available on Windows only.

Choppy video or no sound

In Settings › Streaming, lower the resolution, frame rate or bitrate. If video stutters on a computer with an older graphics card, set Decoder to Software.

Full colour (4:4:4) keeps coloured text and thin lines sharp. It sends about twice the video, so turn it off on a slow connection. Both devices need Secpar 4.2.0 or later.

If there is no sound, check that Play sound here is on and that the right speakers are selected in your system's sound settings.

Updates and errors

The download button at the top of the app shows when an update is available. Opening it checks again, and Secpar also checks every hour. Updating Windows restarts hosting, which ends any stream from that computer.

When a connection fails, the message shows above the computers. The copy button next to it copies the details. Include them, the app version and both devices' systems when you report an issue ↗. Do not share passwords.
