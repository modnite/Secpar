Setup

Help ↗
  1. 1

    Install

    Install Secpar on your computers and phone.

  2. 2

    Sign in

    Sign in with the same account on each device. Your computers appear under Computers.

  3. 3

    Connect

    Select a computer to connect.

In the app

  • Your computers

    Every computer signed in to your account, with a still of its screen and who is using it. Up to three devices can stream from one computer at once.

  • Friends

    Add friends by username and select their computers to connect. For each friend, choose Ask first or Any time, and allow keyboard, mouse and files separately.

  • Files and clipboard

    Browse a computer's files and copy them either way. Text you copy is shared while you are connected.

  • Screens

    Switch between a computer's screens, or open another screen in its own window. Windows computers with no monitor can add a virtual screen at the size you choose.

  • Full colour

    Turn on Full colour (4:4:4) for sharper coloured text. It sends about twice the video.

  • Sound and controllers

    Sound from the computer plays on the device you connect from. Controllers on your phone or computer work on the remote computer.

  • Android TV

    The Android app also runs on Android TV and Chromecast with Google TV, with the remote moving through the app and the stream menu.

  • Power

    Lock, sleep, sign out, restart or shut down your computers from your phone.

  • Privacy

    Turn off previews and no device or friend gets a still of your screen.
